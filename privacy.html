Phonix

Privacy Policy - Last updated 25 September 2026

Overview

Phonix is a phonics reading game for children. Your privacy and your child's privacy are important to us. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data Collection

Phonix does not collect or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is required
  • No personal information is collected
  • No data is sent to any server
  • No analytics or tracking is used
  • No third-party SDKs are included
  • No advertising is displayed

Data Stored on Device

The app stores player profiles (a name and avatar chosen by the user) and reading progress locally on the device only. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on the device, apart from your own iPhone or iPad backups, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

Phonix is designed for children aged 4 to 6. The app complies with COPPA and other children's privacy regulations. No data about children is collected or shared.

Third-Party Services

Phonix does not use any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
